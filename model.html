Plan, as you walk in: the cubbies are straight across on the brick wall and run the full length. The left (west) wall is brick with the glass fridges. The right (east) wall has the whiteboards. The door wall holds the SABA, the crates, and the camera cabinet, whose doors face the cubbies. Positions along the run are measured from the cubbies' left end.

      
The video estimates got horizontal sizes right and overstated vertical ones by about 27% (opening height 13.5 in estimated vs 9.85 measured, unit 30 vs 22). Every vertical value still unmeasured is scaled by that 0.73 factor and marked Estimated.

      
Still unmeasured: total room length, where the run starts relative to the west wall, the counter-to-cubby gap, and the cubby depth. Each is marked Estimated below.
